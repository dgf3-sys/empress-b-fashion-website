QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Choose a design or send us your own inspiration, then click the WhatsApp button to discuss your order with us.

Can I send my own design?

Yes. You can send an inspiration picture and explain exactly what you would like us to create.

Do you make custom sizes?

Yes. Custom orders can be discussed based on your measurements and preferred fit.

Can I ask for the price before ordering?

Yes. Click the enquiry button and ask for the current price of the design you are interested in.

Do I pay directly on the website?

No online payment is required through this website. Orders and payment arrangements can be discussed directly with Empress B Stitches.

How do I contact Empress B Stitches?

You can contact us through WhatsApp or email using the contact details at the bottom of this website.
